Common Questions About the ShareThings Ecosystem

What is ShareThings and what applications are included?

ShareThings is a modular ecosystem of commercial and family automation tools: including ReplyFlow (24/7 Fanpage AI assistant, order closing, and phone number privacy sentry), BabyGuard AI (smart pediatric nursery sentry with 100% on-device privacy), and VidFlow (automated multi-platform video publishing).

Do I ever need to share personal Facebook account passwords?

Never! All ShareThings applications connect strictly through Meta’s official Graph API business protocols. You never share personal passwords or bank credentials.

How does the automated payment and activation mechanism work?

Our infrastructure connects directly to automated payment gateways. Upon scanning the QR code with the designated memo, licenses unlock automatically within 3–5 seconds without manual intervention.

How is customer data and business revenue protected?

We strictly comply with Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree 13/2023/ND-CP, Meta Platform Terms, and undergo regular security evaluations compliant with OWASP Top 10 standards. We guarantee never selling or sharing your business data with third parties.
