User Data Deletion Instructions

Standard operating procedure for requesting user data erasure compliant with Meta Platform Terms and Vietnam Decree 13/2023/ND-CP.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: November 04, 2026

1. Disconnect ReplyFlow in Facebook Business Settings

Fanpage administrators can immediately revoke ReplyFlow access directly via Facebook:

  1. Log in to your Facebook personal account that administers the Fanpage.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click Business Integrations.
  4. Locate ReplyFlow, check the box, and click Remove.

Once removed, ReplyFlow will immediately stop receiving new webhook events and messages from your Fanpage.

2. Request Complete Data Erasure

To permanently erase all historical data stored in our systems, submit an erasure request via email to nvtthanh11@gmail.com, Telegram @nvtthanh11, or WhatsApp/Zalo +84 908 674 036 with the following details:

  • For Fanpage Administrators: Provide your Page ID, Page name, and a statement requesting full data deletion.
  • For End-User Consumers: Provide the name of the Fanpage you messaged along with your Facebook public display name.
