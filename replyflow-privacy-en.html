Privacy Policy

Transparent description of how ReplyFlow processes, safeguards, and handles Facebook Page and Messenger Platform Data.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: November 04, 2026

1. Scope & Meta Platform Compliance

This Privacy Policy describes how ReplyFlow (a product within the ShareThings ecosystem) collects, processes, stores, and protects data when integrated with Facebook Fanpages via Meta’s official Messenger API and Webhooks. We strictly adhere to:

  • Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies.
  • Decree 13/2023/ND-CP of the Government of Vietnam on Personal Data Protection.
  • Law on Protection of Consumer Rights 2023 and international data security standards.

2. Data We Collect

  • Fanpage Information: Page ID, Page name, and Page access tokens authorized by the administrator.
  • Messenger Communications: Page-Scoped User ID (PSID provided by Meta, not personal Facebook account ID), public display name, message timestamps, and message contents.
  • Public Comments: Public comments left on Page posts, used solely to respond and automatically hide comments containing customer phone numbers per administrator configuration.
  • Customer-Initiated Images: Images voluntarily sent by customers (e.g., payment receipts) to verify order transactions.
  • Order Fulfillment Information: Voluntarily provided details: customer name, shipping address, and phone number.
  • Service Billing Information: Merchant payment records: amount, timestamp, and transfer memo. We never store banking passwords or credit card numbers.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Facebook personal account passwords or any credential data.
  • Friends lists, personal timeline newsfeeds, or private user data outside the authorized Page scope.
  • Sensitive biometric or health personal data.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Answering product inquiries, consulting customers, and logging orders on behalf of the Page administrator.
  • Masking public phone numbers in comments to protect consumer privacy and prevent lead poaching.
  • Verifying customer bank transfer payments.
  • Customer care messaging strictly within Meta’s standard messaging policies.
  • Compiling operational summaries and analytics for the Fanpage administrator.
  • Service activation, renewal, and technical support.

We never sell customer data, do not share data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes, and never utilize private customer conversations to train public AI foundation models.

5. Security & Data Storage

Data is stored in secured relational databases, protected by multi-layer firewalls, and encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HSTS. Administrative access is restricted with strict role-based access control (RBAC). For DIAMOND Self-Hosted installations, 100% of data resides entirely on the customer’s private machine.

6. User Data Deletion & Your Rights

Both Fanpage administrators and messaging consumers have the absolute right to request inspection, modification, or complete deletion of their data at any time:

  • Revoking App Permissions: Administrators can revoke access immediately via Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations → Remove ReplyFlow.
  • Permanent Data Erasure: To delete all stored conversation history and database records, visit our Data Deletion Guide or email nvtthanh11@gmail.com. Requests are permanently executed within 72 hours.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new Meta platform guidelines or statutory amendments. Significant revisions will be prominently notified on our website.
